      The S-T-A-R Response Framework

      The proven method evaluated by Google, Amazon, and Fortune 500 recruiters.

      S - Situation (15%)

      Describe the specific context, project, or problem you faced.

      T - Task (15%)

      Explain your concrete responsibility and what goal was required.

      A - Action (50%)

      Detail the specific steps YOU took, tools chosen, and decisions made.

      R - Result (20%)

      Quantify outcomes, metrics improved, delivery dates, and takeaways.
